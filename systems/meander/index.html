§ 01 · Reading

Paper carries the words

Blue bands carry the loud, short things — hero, stats, actions, the footer. Body copy, lists and tables live on paper: white ground, blue ink. Bands alternate as full-bleed horizontal seams, and the drawn meander marks each transition. This is a drawn ornament, not a scanline.

  1. Semantic tokens only — never a raw hex in page markup.
  2. One accent per system, the acid yellow, spent sparingly.
  3. Drawn rules at two-pixel pottery weight, never a hairline.
  4. The text surface always wins over the field behind it.

Status note. Errors stay editorial: blue ink on paper with a yellow underline. No dashboard chrome.
