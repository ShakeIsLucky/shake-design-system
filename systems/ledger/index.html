Ledger

Racing-green rules on ivory stock · Freight + IBM Plex Mono · one gold stamp · zero radius.

Everything Racing-Green does with green surfaces, Ledger does with green structure. The palette, the type, the bracketed gold and the hangtag rail move onto a light, readable field. Green frames the page and carries the voice; the reading column stays ivory, and the ink reads green-black.

§ callout · the take

Green is structure, never the reading ground

A callout is the one place a page raises its voice. Ledger sets it on a faint parchment inset, ruled green top and bottom, with a single gold stripe down the edge. The words stay in green-black ink so the take is louder than its box.

The take

Spend green on the frame and gold on the stamp. If either one lands on the reading column, the page is arguing with itself and the reader loses.

Ledger paper was never about the lines. It was about knowing, at a glance, that every entry had a place — and that the total sat where you expected it.

§ stat row · totals

Figures close under a double rule

Monospaced numerals, ruled above with the ledger's double line and closed below with a single. This is a sum, not a dashboard: three figures, quiet, right where the eye goes to check them.
